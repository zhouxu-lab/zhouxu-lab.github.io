Research

Our group develops advanced food processing technologies and computational methods for next-generation food manufacturing. We integrate sensing, multi-physics simulation, machine learning, and robotics to build digital twins of food processes and supply chains. Below are four current research thrusts.

1. AI Agents for Food Applications

Goal

Build AI agent systems tailored for food science research and decision-making.

Approach

Multi-agent architectures (equi-level, hierarchical, nested) coordinating specialized agents for food R&D. Papers under review.

2. Digital Extruder

Goal

Digital twin linking ingredients → process → product attributes for formulation and process optimization.

Approach

Mechanistic multi-physics models coupled with generative AI and ML surrogates for fast prediction and closed-loop optimization.

Microwave-assisted thermal pasteurization (MAPS) digital twin
Radio-frequency (RF) drying: system & simulation

3. Microwave & RF Heating

Goal

Uniform, energy-efficient electromagnetic heating for pasteurization, sterilization, and drying.

Approach

Pilot-scale systems coupled with simulation digital twins (Maxwell + heat transfer) to design and optimize cavities, trays, and processes.

4. Food Supply Chain Digital Twin

Goal

End-to-end visibility and decision support across food cold chains.

Approach

IoT sensors, digital-twin modeling, and analytics to track and optimize food quality, safety, and sustainability.
